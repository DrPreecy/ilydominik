## What the hash chain actually guarantees

With no key and no external anchor: detection of edits or reordering of events *before* some head hash you independently trust. Not detected: tail truncation, appending valid-looking events, full re-hash. That makes it an accident/corruption detector and a "deliberate-forgery speed bump", not evidence against the AI agent it is meant to constrain (O-1).
